Our Story

Brewed in Kenya. Served with love.

A café with a Kenyan heart

Savanna Brew Café is a locally owned coffee shop in Westlands, Nairobi. Opened in 2019 by founder Amina Otieno, the café grew from a lifelong love of Kenyan coffee.

Amina grew up on her grandparents’ coffee farm in Nyeri. Those early experiences inspired her to celebrate Kenya’s coffee heritage and create a welcoming place where people can enjoy quality coffee and freshly baked snacks.

We support local farmers, use eco-friendly packaging and host monthly community events. Every cup is an opportunity to connect people with the people and places behind their coffee.

Our Values

Quality

Freshly roasted, single-origin Kenyan beans and carefully prepared treats.

Community

Fair prices for farmers and a welcoming space for everyone.

Sustainability

Compostable cups, responsible packaging and zero-waste pastries.

“Our dream is simple: to celebrate Kenyan coffee while creating a place where everyone feels at home.” — Amina Otieno
